AANO Notes

A minimalist, offline-first, and privacy-focused note-taking application for Android and Windows Desktop.

Key Features

  • Offline-First Storage: All notes, folders, and archives are stored locally on your device first.
  • User-Owned Google Drive Sync: Optional synchronization across Android and Windows using your own Google Drive (drive.file scope — AANO only accesses its own aano-notes.json sync file).
  • Zero Telemetry & 100% Privacy: Licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later with client-side AES-256 encryption. No trackers or third-party ad SDKs.
  • Rich Text & Custom Themes: Full WYSIWYG editor with headings, colors, highlighters, clickable links, and trash bin.
